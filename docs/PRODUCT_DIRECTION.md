# SAWT AI — Product Direction

## Product promise
A simple Arabic-first speech studio for natural, accurate Modern Standard Arabic (الفصحى), designed to work without paid per-character services whenever on-device models can support it.

## Required Arabic voice profiles
The primary Arabic catalogue must include these two distinct voices:
1. **Male — deep Fusha narrator**: mature, low-register, steady and warm; clear articulation and formal Modern Standard Arabic, not a regional dialect.
2. **Female — clear Fusha narrator**: clear, confident, smooth and natural; formal Modern Standard Arabic, not a regional dialect.

The voice labels describe target delivery, not a claim that a model has passed quality assurance. Do not label a voice as Fusha, male/female, offline, or commercially redistributable until the underlying voice and its license have been checked.

## Product principles
1. Arabic-first, with Modern Standard Arabic as the default Arabic language; no dialect voices in the Fusha catalogue.
2. The male and female voice profiles above are required product choices, not optional device fallbacks.
3. Prefer downloadable on-device voice packs so text can be synthesized privately, without a provider account, per-character fee, or permanent network connection.
4. Show pack size, language, voice profile, model version, license/attribution, and verification status before download.
5. Keep voice packs optional and downloadable after installation; do not make the APK unreasonably large.
6. Playback and MP3/WAV export must work for text generated by a verified local model.
7. Do not claim unlimited free cloud generation. Any cloud mode must clearly disclose that it requires a paid/limited provider or funded compute.
8. Never silently fall back to a device's installed TTS voice and present it as the SAWT Fusha voice.
9. Never include provider secrets in the Android APK.

## Model evaluation notes (not yet an app implementation)
- Nabra-7M-Distill is a promising small, offline MSA female-voice candidate (28.7 MB, Apache-2.0 according to its model card). It is explicitly single-voice and its published inference package is Python/Kokoro-based; Android runtime conversion and listening QA are still required.
- A matching small, commercially redistributable, verified MSA male model has not yet been approved.
- Larger open models that provide voice design or multiple speakers may be evaluated, but their storage, RAM, speed, Android compatibility, and license must be checked on real target phones before selection.
- A research/non-commercial-only model must not be used in a public app or commercial distribution.

## Roadmap
- Phase 1: stable Android shell and Arabic editor.
- Phase 2: verify and integrate one deep male and one clear female MSA on-device model, including Android runtime and download packs.
- Phase 3: audio export, long-text queue, document import, and audio library.
- Phase 4: optional cloud mode only when its provider, funding, privacy notice, limits, and abuse protections are explicit.
- Phase 5: low-memory/older-device profiling, accessibility, app localization, and release QA.

## Quality gates
- Build and lint pass.
- Listen to identical benchmark passages with native Arabic reviewers.
- Check ع، ح، ص، ض، ط، ظ، ث، ذ، ق, hamza, tanween, tashkeel, numbers, names, and punctuation.
- Verify that the male voice sounds naturally male and deep without artificial pitch-shifting.
- Verify that the female voice sounds naturally female and clear.
- Test a low-memory Android device and a mid-range device, both offline after voice-pack download.
- Confirm the complete license chain and attribution obligations for code, weights, voice data, and any reference recordings.
- Do not advertise a voice as fully verified until these checks pass.
